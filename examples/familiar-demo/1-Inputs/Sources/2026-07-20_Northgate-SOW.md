SCOPE OF WORK

Between: Meridian Consulting Partners ("Meridian") and Northgate Freight ("Client")
Date: July 20, 2026
Engagement: Depot Consolidation Feasibility Study

1. BACKGROUND

Northgate Freight operates three regional depots (Hartsdale, Millbrook, Coldwater) serving its
regional trucking and last-mile delivery network. The Client has asked Meridian to assess whether
consolidating operations into a single depot would reduce fixed operating cost without materially
degrading regional delivery coverage.

2. SCOPE OF DELIVERABLES

2.1 Depot consolidation feasibility report, covering: current-state cost baseline per depot,
consolidation scenarios (single-site and two-site options), delivery coverage impact modeling, and
a risk register.

2.2 Cost/savings model comparing status-quo operation against each consolidation scenario, in
Excel, with documented assumptions.

2.3 Client-facing recommendation deck summarizing findings and a recommended path, for presentation
to the Northgate executive team.

3. TIMELINE

Kickoff: July 20, 2026. Draft feasibility report delivered to Client: September 15, 2026. Final
recommendation presentation: September 30, 2026. Engagement window: 10 weeks from kickoff.

4. OUT OF SCOPE

Fleet routing software selection is out of scope. Unionized labour negotiation strategy is out of
scope; Northgate's own legal counsel retains responsibility for that workstream.

5. BUDGET

Fixed-fee engagement, billed at two milestones (kickoff, delivery). Additional budget may be sought
if the Client requests more than two review rounds on the feasibility report, or if the engagement
timeline extends beyond the 10-week window stated above.

6. KEY CONTACTS

Northgate: Dana Whitfield, Chief Operating Officer (engagement sponsor and decision-maker). Marcus
Ilunga, VP Operations (day-to-day contact).

Meridian: Engagement lead and one analyst assigned to this engagement.

7. NOTE ON DEPOT 3

The Coldwater depot (Depot 3) operates under a facility lease with the current landlord. Northgate
has flagged that any consolidation scenario touching Depot 3 needs to account for the lease's
renewal decision point, since a lapsed renewal window would force a decision on Depot 3's future
independent of this study's own timeline. Exact date to be confirmed by Northgate Facilities and
provided at kickoff.
