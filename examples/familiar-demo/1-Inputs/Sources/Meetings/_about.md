# Meetings

Notes, minutes, and transcripts of meetings that already happened. Planned meetings live in
`4-Trackers/meetings.md`; this folder is the after-the-fact record.

Name files `YYYY-MM-DD_meeting-name.md` (or the original extension), dated by the meeting, not the
upload. When the meeting is in `4-Trackers/meetings.md`, use its name so the two can be matched.

Immutable: familiar reads from these but never edits, rewrites, or moves them. Ingesting one follows
`.familiar/core/ingest.md` → Meeting notes: the page records what was decided, who owns each action,
and what was left open, and familiar proposes the tracker and decision updates rather than making them.
