SCOPE OF WORK

Between: Meridian Consulting Partners ("Meridian") and Cauldron Coffee Roasters ("Client")
Date: August 3, 2026
Engagement: Competitor Research Brief (Daybreak Coffee Co.)

1. BACKGROUND

Cauldron Coffee Roasters is a regional specialty roaster with eleven cafes, a wholesale arm and a
home-delivery subscription. Daybreak Coffee Co., a national chain, has announced it is entering
Cauldron's home region. The Client has asked Meridian for a research brief on Daybreak so its
leadership can decide how to respond.

2. SCOPE OF DELIVERABLES

2.1 A competitor research brief on Daybreak, covering: who they are and how they are growing, their
regional launch plan, their offer and pricing against Cauldron's, their positioning, and what it
means for Cauldron.

2.2 A one-page executive summary of the brief.

2.3 A 30-minute readout of the findings to the Cauldron leadership team.

3. TIMELINE

Kickoff: August 10, 2026. Draft brief delivered to Client: September 14, 2026. Final brief and
readout: September 21, 2026. Engagement window: 6 weeks from kickoff.

4. OUT OF SCOPE

Recommendations on Cauldron's own pricing or product range are out of scope. Primary customer
research (surveys, interviews) is out of scope; the brief uses published and publicly observable
information only.

5. BUDGET

Fixed-fee engagement, billed at two milestones (kickoff, delivery). Additional budget may be sought
if the Client requests more than two review rounds on the draft brief, or if the engagement runs
beyond the 6-week window stated above.

6. KEY CONTACTS

Cauldron: Hannah Brooks, Head of Strategy (engagement sponsor and decision-maker). Tomas Reyes,
Marketing Manager (day-to-day contact).

Meridian: Engagement lead and one analyst assigned to this engagement.

7. NOTE ON DAYBREAK'S LAUNCH

Daybreak has announced its first stores in the region but the Client has not confirmed an opening
date. The Client has noted that the first weeks after an opening are when customers choose where to
go, so the brief needs to land close to that date. Exact date to be confirmed by the Client at
kickoff.
