Download one folder, give it your project's name, and open your AI coding agent inside it. Every
session then starts from the project's record instead of from nothing.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Remembers the project" icon="document">
    Status, tasks, decisions and handoffs live as Markdown in the folder. A new session reads them and
    picks up where the last one stopped.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Answers from your sources" icon="magnifier">
    Ingested documents become cited pages with a locator on every claim, so answers trace back to your
    own files rather than the model's memory.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Workflows built in" icon="rocket">
    Check-ins, ingest, deliverable briefs, Word, PowerPoint and Excel drafts, research and handoffs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free, open, yours" icon="open-book">
    Plain Markdown and a few small scripts. No account, no server, nothing to sign up for. Nothing is
    stored outside the folder.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Across all your projects

The optional [fleet hub](https://github.com/familiar-md/familiar-hub) reads every project and writes
you a daily brief: what is overdue, what lands in the next two weeks, and what you can start today.

Works with coding agents that open a folder: built and tested with Claude Code, also used with Codex
and Gemini CLI. Not for Claude.ai chat or Claude Cowork.
