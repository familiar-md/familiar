# "Depot Consolidation Trends Among Mid-Size Regional Carriers"

## Summary
Fictional industry market note (June 2026) on depot consolidation patterns among mid-size regional
trucking carriers. External benchmark for this engagement's cost-savings modeling and scenario
design — not a Northgate-specific source.

## Key findings
- Consolidation reviews are typically triggered by a facility lease expiry forcing the question,
  rather than by proactive network redesign [Market Note, ¶1].
- Three-to-two-depot consolidations: average fixed-cost savings 8–14%, concentrated in facility
  lease/utilities and site admin headcount, partly offset by higher line-haul cost [Market Note,
  ¶2].
- Single-site (three-to-one) consolidations: wider outcome range, 8–22% savings, but three
  documented cases of delivery-window degradation severe enough to cause customer churn [Market
  Note, ¶2].
- Carriers modeling **only** a single-site scenario were more likely to hit the delivery-coverage
  degradation outcome than carriers that modeled single- and two-site scenarios in parallel, which
  caught capacity shortfalls before implementation [Market Note, ¶3].
- Facility lease timing is an underweighted risk factor: several cases describe an individual site's
  own lease deadline forcing a standalone decision on that site ahead of the broader consolidation
  study's conclusions [Market Note, ¶4].
