# Decision — Scope the brief to Daybreak's regional launch

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Track:** shared
**Status:** active
**Decided by:** Client (Hannah Brooks, Cauldron Head of Strategy), overriding Meridian's original
proposal

## Context
Meridian's original proposal covered Daybreak's whole national business, on the assumption that
leadership would want the full picture. At kickoff, Hannah was firm that leadership only needs to
know what Daybreak will do in Cauldron's region [`2026-08-10_Kickoff-Notes.md`, §2 Decisions Made].

## Options considered
- **National business (Meridian's original proposal).** More background, but more research time and a
  longer brief that leadership would have to read past.
- **Regional launch and subscription offer only (client-directed, chosen).** Shorter and faster, and
  focused on the part of Daybreak that competes with Cauldron.

## Decision
The brief covers Daybreak's regional launch and its subscription offer. National figures appear only
as context where they explain the regional plan.

## Consequences
Keeps the research track to a small set of questions and lets the brief land close to Daybreak's
September 12 opening. Rules out a section on Daybreak's national strategy.

## What would reopen this
If leadership asks for a view of Daybreak's national plans, or if the grocery and wholesale channel
(an open question from kickoff) turns out to matter in the region.
